title: The commit box comes up the screen, and the terms wait until you ask
---

The screen where you name a goal opened with a 90-word paragraph doing five jobs
at once, and the box you came for sat underneath it — a quarter of a phone
screen spent on terms, in front of somebody who had just signed in and typed
nothing. Two sentences stay above the box now, and they are the two the commit
is actually a commitment to: pick the problem you'll test rather than the idea
you'll finish, and it costs one task each morning and proof of it each evening.
The rest — that he coaches one thing at a time, that the first ask is one evening
at your desk, that you can close it whenever you like and an idea that dies in
front of real people still reads as tested — is a tap away under *What you're
agreeing to*, which is where you'd go looking for it if you were hesitating.
Nothing was deleted and nothing about the goal changed; the words that answer a
question just stopped arriving as terms.
