title: A nudge on your phone if tonight's proof is still owed
---

The whole of this product is "come back tonight", and until now the only thing
holding you to that was remembering a browser tab. There is now a switch at the
foot of the Today card: turn it on, and if you declared a task this morning and
the proof box is still empty once your evening has started, Masterji sends one
notification with your own words in it. Once a day, never twice, and only if
you asked for it. Nothing about it touches the gate — it cannot bank a day,
refuse one or move a phase, and a day you never declared on gets no
notification at all, because there is nothing there to hold you to. Tapping it
opens the box, and if the app is already open it goes to the tab you had rather
than a new one, so a half-written proof survives. It works on Android and on a
desktop straight away; on iPhone it needs the app added to your home screen
first, and the switch says so instead of pretending.
