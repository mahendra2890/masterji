title: A sign-in only finishes in the browser that started it
---

Signing in with Google now has to end in the same browser it began in, not
merely in one holding a link Masterji handed out. Nothing changes for you if
you sign in normally. If a sign-in takes longer than ten minutes at Google's
account picker, or your cookies go away in the middle of it, you land back on
the front page with the sign-in panel open and a note saying so — one more
press and you are in.
