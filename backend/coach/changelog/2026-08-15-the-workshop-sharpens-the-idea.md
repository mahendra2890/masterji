title: The workshop sharpens the idea now, not just picks it
---

The room before your goal used to stop the moment you had one candidate worth
committing to — and IDEA's four questions were left for your first morning,
which meant re-answering things you had already spent the conversation on. Now
those four questions stand on the left of the screen from your first turn in
the room, and fill in as you answer them: the problem, the one place those
people already are, why you think they're there, and how you'd get one
conversation this week. Masterji works through the open ones with you, and what
lands carries over so your first morning starts from what you already told him.
The room is twenty turns instead of fifteen, because that is a longer
conversation than just choosing. It still refuses nothing: Commit works from
the first turn and at nought of four exactly as it does at four of four — while
the four are open the questions are simply the loudest thing on the screen.
