title: The workshop counts how much of the first phase you can already write
---

The room before the goal now keeps a count. As Masterji digs into the
candidate you are circling — who has the problem, which room those people are
in, why you think they are there, how you would get one conversation this week
— the workshop shows how many of those four you could already write down
tonight, and names the ones still open. Those four are exactly what IDEA asks
for on the other side of the commit, so the number is a forecast of what
committing would cost, not a score. It gates nothing: Commit works at nought
of four the same as it does at four, nothing is banked, and IDEA's first proof
is still yours to file and still judged after you are in.
