title: A link to a closed record, for the people who ask what you actually did
---

Open any closed idea and there is a new button: get a link. It opens a page
anyone can read without an account — the phase you reached, how many proofs you
banked, how many of those came from talking to real people, days active, your
longest run, and the dates each phase opened. An idea that died in front of
real people reads as "tested → dead" on it, which is the only version of "it
didn't work out" that says something true about you. Every number on the page
came through a gate, and the page says so.

What is not on it: the sentence you wrote when you closed it, anything Masterji
said, any proof text, any day's work, and your name. It is off until you press
the button, and turning it off takes the link away for good — switching it back
on gives you a different one, so a link you regret stops working.
