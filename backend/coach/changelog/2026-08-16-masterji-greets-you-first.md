title: On a phone, Masterji greets you first
---

The dashboard used to open on the Today card, with the conversation one tap
away. On a phone it now opens on the conversation — since drafts complete and
commit right in the chat, that is where a day usually starts. Today has not
moved: same pill, one tap, and its badges still tell you when a draft, notes,
or an open day is waiting there. On a computer nothing changed; both halves
are on screen at once, as before.
