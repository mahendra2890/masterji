title: TRACTION finally has a playbook about people coming back
---

TRACTION asks for one person who returned on their own, or who paid — and the
only playbook loaded there taught recruiting users one at a time, which is
acquisition. Masterji now also carries *Coming Back*, distilled from Andrew
Chen's writing on retention and the leaky bucket: why somebody returns at all
(it is caused by what happened last time, not by what you shipped this week),
how to tell a real return from a second first-time visitor when you have ten
users and no analytics, and the difference between coming back and being
reminded — which look the same in a count and mean opposite things, so if you
nudged them, the record should say so. It also names what not to reach for.
Notifications, streaks, badges and points manufacture the look of a return
without the reason for one, and at this size they hide the only signal you
have. The bar has not moved: the same person, back a second time, or a payment.
