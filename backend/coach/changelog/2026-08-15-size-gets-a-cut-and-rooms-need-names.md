title: A goal that feels too big gets a cut, not the exit — and a route is graded on its room, never its app
---

Two coach replies got more careful. Asking whether your goal is too big now
gets an answer about size: where to cut, that doing the small bar in front of
you is what shows where it is actually too wide, and that the reword control
on your goal card can take a narrower wording while nothing is banked yet.
What it no longer gets is an unasked-for reminder that the close box exists —
Masterji only offers to open that box once you say you want out. Closing
itself has not moved: it is still free, still yours, still one honest sentence
away. And in IDEA, your route is now judged on one thing — whether it names a
real room a stranger could find — never on which app or platform the room
lives in. The same answer gets the same push-back wherever you say it lives,
and if the kind of room you name does not exist on the platform you name,
Masterji will say that plainly instead of letting the platform's name do the
grading. No gate changed either way.
