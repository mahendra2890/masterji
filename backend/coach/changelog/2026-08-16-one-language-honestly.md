title: One language, said honestly — the EN/हिं switch is gone

The switch in the header offered a language Masterji did not reliably speak.
In the room before your goal he answered in Hinglish; in the daily chat, where
you actually spend the week, he answered in English whichever side you had
pressed. A control that changes nothing is worse than no control, so it has
gone, and Masterji is English everywhere. Nothing about your goal, your record,
your streak or the gate changes, and the header has a little more room on a
phone. If Hinglish comes back it comes back properly — in both rooms, not in
one.
