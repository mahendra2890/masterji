title: Name the hour, not just the task
---

Declaring now takes an optional hour: "by 21:00", beside the Declare it
button. It goes on the record with the task, and Masterji reads it back — so
he can hold you to a time you picked yourself instead of a vague "tonight".
Nothing else changes. It is not a deadline: a proof filed at 23:40 against a
named 21:00 counts exactly as much, your streak is untouched, and no phase gate
looks at it. Leave it on "any time tonight" and your day works precisely as it
did before.
