title: A day you ran twice reads as two days no more
---

Running a second cycle in one day was always allowed, and the record could not
show it: two rows, one date, nothing else to tell them apart. Repeats are now
numbered — 2nd, 3rd — and a day's cycles are ordered newest first like the rest
of the card. The phase drill-in also takes its dates from the rows it is
showing, so its heading can no longer name a range those rows fall outside, and
it says how many days and cycles are under it.
