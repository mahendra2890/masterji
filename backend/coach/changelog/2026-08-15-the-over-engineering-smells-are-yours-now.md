title: The over-engineering smells are the ones a first project actually commits
---

Masterji's field guide to over-engineering listed six smells with Kubernetes
and permissions matrices at the top — things you reach for after a few years
inside a software company, not things a first product dies of. The list is
reordered to the order a first project actually meets them: tool polishing and
the rewrite at the top, infrastructure at the bottom. Three smells that were
missing are in it now — choosing a framework you have never used as the
project, rebuilding the UI because it looks amateur next to products with
design teams, and building an app where a link you can paste into WhatsApp
would do. The counter-question and the section on the second idea are
unchanged.
