title: A bad afternoon at the model provider no longer takes the whole app with it
---

Masterji has always had an answer for a model that will not reply: the proof
still lands, the day still counts, and the verdict says so. The problem was how
long that took. Every request waited out the full timeout before reaching it, and
enough of them at once and nothing else on the site could get a turn either.

Now a request has an overall time limit rather than just a per-call one, and
after a few failures in a row Masterji stops waiting and goes straight to the
honest answer. Nothing changes on a normal day. On a bad one you get the same
verdict you would have got, sooner, and the rest of the app keeps working.
