title: The app opens on its own frame instead of a blank screen
---

Opening Masterji when you were already signed in used to mean waiting on a
blank page: the browser had to fetch the whole app and ask the server who you
were before it could draw a single thing. Now the page arrives with your
dashboard's frame already on it — the header, both columns, the cards — and
fills in as your goal and today's task load. Nothing jumps around while that
happens, which it used to. If you are not signed in, the landing page is
unchanged, and it now downloads noticeably less to show you.
