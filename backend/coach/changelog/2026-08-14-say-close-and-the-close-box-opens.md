title: Say you want out in the chat and the close box opens — Masterji can no longer tell you a goal is closed
---

Closing a goal has always been free, and Masterji has always been allowed to
say so. What he could not do was close one — and nothing stopped him saying he
had. Tell him you were done and he could answer "Done. This goal is closed."
over a goal that was still running, with nothing written and nothing on the
record. That sentence is gone: he cannot close a goal, he is told so, and he
will not claim it. What he can do now is open the door for you. Say you want
out and the close box opens on your goal card, scrolled to and waiting — and
it still asks you for the two things that were always yours: one honest
sentence about what happened, which goes on the record, and which way it
ended. Nothing closes until you write that sentence and press an exit
yourself. He only ever opens it when you have asked to get out; a bad week is
not an answer to a question you did not ask.
