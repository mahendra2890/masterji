title: Talk the launch day down to a Friday in chat and it's waiting in the box
---

Once you're building, the day you'll launch is something you argue down from
"someday" to an actual Friday in the conversation — and then you used to leave
the chat, open the launch box, and pick that same day and room again by hand.
Now Masterji writes down the day and the room you named, they appear above the
launch box in your words, and one tap fills both controls. He only writes down a
day you said or agreed to yourself: there is still no default date and no
suggested one, because a day the app picked is not a day you committed to.
Nothing is on the record until you press Set — the launch trail is append-only
and every move on it is one you made, so a drafted day never writes a row. Set
still checks everything it always did: not a day that has been, inside the next
three months, and a real room off the ladder. The box works on its own exactly
as before — type the day yourself, ignore the draft, or move the date later.
