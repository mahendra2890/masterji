title: The idea screen's two columns now match
---

On a wide screen, the column that holds your commit box and the conversation
beside it are now two panels in the same frame, standing the same height —
instead of a card next to a loose stack that stopped wherever it ran out.
The "What IDEA will ask you for" panel has an even shape now, and its heading
reads as a sentence: IDEA kept its capitals because it is the phase's name,
and the rest of the line stopped shouting. Nothing moved on the phone, and
nothing about what the screen does changed.
