title: You can reword today's task, and he'll suggest the sharper version
---

When Masterji read this morning's task and called it too vague or off-phase,
the only button under him said "File tonight's proof" — a complaint you could
do nothing about until tomorrow. Two things change. There is now a "reword
today's task" control on the card, so the wording of today's work is yours to
sharpen at any point before you file the proof. And where he objected, he now
writes the sharper version of your own task underneath, one press away from the
box. It stays your task and your call: take his wording, write your own, or
leave it exactly as it is. Nothing is refused, and nothing is rewritten without
you pressing Declare it.
