title: Your cohort, ranked on the ledger rather than on the deck
---

If your E-Cell runs a cohort, they can give you a join code, and *Your cohort*
becomes a page: everyone who joined, ranked on what the server counted. Proofs
that were accepted, how many of those needed a real person, your current
streak, the phase you reached. Nothing on that board is self-reported —
there is no field on it anybody can write in. It does not show your idea, your
brief, a single word of any proof, or your email; a coordinator sees the counts
and nothing you typed. Joining is the whole of the agreement, and until you
join a cohort you are invisible to every part of this: not listed, not ranked,
not counted in a total. Leaving takes your row off the board the same evening
and changes nothing else — every goal, every morning you declared and evening
you proved stays exactly as it is, because your record does not know a cohort
exists. And nobody running one can bank or unbank anything. There is no button
for it, and the page they read has no way to write at all. That is the point:
a leaderboard of decks measures who presents best, and this one can only ever
show what got through the gate.
