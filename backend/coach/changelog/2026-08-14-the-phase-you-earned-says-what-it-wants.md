title: The phase you just earned now says what it wants
---

Unlocking a phase used to be five words — "Phase unlocked: VALIDATION → BUILD."
Signing up, by contrast, gets a full briefing: what IDEA is for, what to write,
and what waits until later. That was the wrong way round, because the phases you
earn are the bigger jump. BUILD arrives wanting two nights of evidence with at
least one of them a real person touching the thing. LAUNCH wants three nights in
front of strangers, one of them somebody actually acting. TRACTION wants one
stranger who came back on their own, or who paid. Every unlock now carries a
short brief from Masterji saying so — the count that phase will ask you for, and
the mistake it is most often lost to — distilled from that phase's own
playbooks, which are all in the public repository as they always have been.
Nothing about any gate moved: the same proofs cost the same, and a refusal says
exactly what it said before. Being told precisely what is still missing is the
coaching at that moment, and it is not the moment for a briefing about a phase
you have not opened yet.
