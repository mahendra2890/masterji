title: The workshop looks like the conversation it always was
---

The room before the goal was drawn in a different language from the chat you
get after committing: Masterji had no face in it, his replies were boxed and
yours were filled — the opposite of everywhere else — the text was smaller, and
the box you typed into was two fixed rows that could not grow. All of that is
now the same as the chat's, because it is the same conversation. On a wide
screen the room sits beside the commit box instead of in a 320px window under
it, with the parked candidates and the count of what you could already write
next to the box they fill. On a phone the commit box stays pinned to the top,
so every "put it in the box above" is about something you can actually see.
Nothing about the room's terms moved: fifteen turns, three candidates, Send is
still the quiet button and Commit is still the only loud one.
