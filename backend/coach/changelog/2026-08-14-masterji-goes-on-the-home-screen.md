title: Masterji installs to your home screen
---

Masterji can now be added to your phone's home screen, where it opens as its
own app — no address bar, no hunting for the tab you left open on Tuesday. On
Android the browser menu offers **Install**; on an iPhone it is **Share → Add
to Home Screen**. The icon is the same **म** that stands in for Masterji on
every message in the chat. Nothing else changes: it is the same app, the same
account and the same record, one tap closer. Notifications are not part of
this — the app still only speaks when you open it.
