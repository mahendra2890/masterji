title: Waking Masterji up takes less of your time
---

The backend still sleeps when nobody has needed it for a while — that part is
the price of free hosting and it has not changed. What has changed is how long
it takes to come back. It used to be about two minutes; it is now about half a
minute, because it runs somewhere that gives it a whole processor to start on
rather than a tenth of one, and no longer stops to check its own database on
the way up.
