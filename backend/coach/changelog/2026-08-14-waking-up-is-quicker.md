title: Waking Masterji up takes less of your time
---

The backend still sleeps after fifteen quiet minutes — that part is the price
of free hosting and it has not changed. What has changed is what it does when
you knock. It was rebuilding a large part of itself from scratch on every
single wake, and now it ships ready to run, so the note that holds the door
clears sooner than it used to.
