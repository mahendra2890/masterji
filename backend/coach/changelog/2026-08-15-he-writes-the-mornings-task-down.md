title: Masterji writes this morning's task down from what you told him
---

He has written tonight's proof from your conversation for a while. The morning
was the half you still typed twice: work out today's task over four turns in
chat, close it, scroll to Today, type it again from memory. Now when you say
what you're doing today, it's in the box waiting for you — in your words, as he
heard them. Nothing is declared until you press the button, exactly as before:
he writes it down, you commit to it. If the wording isn't right, edit it first.
