title: The bar turns green when you meet it, and the phase you cleared is named on the next one
---

At 3/3 the meter was the same marigold it was at 1/3. The one thing on the card
you can read at a glance said nothing at the best moment in the product, while
the line directly under it had been spending the good colour on that exact state
all along. The bar now goes green when the phase is met — and only then: a full
count with a kind still owed is not a met bar, and the meter says so by staying
marigold while the card names what's left.

The other half is what happens one press later. Opening the next phase turned a
full bar and *Earned* into `0/2 proofs toward LAUNCH` over an empty one, with
the advance button back. Three real conversations became a new debt, and nothing
on the screen said the three were still on the record. They are, and the card
now says so: **3 proofs from VALIDATION stay on the record**, under the new
phase's empty bar, until that phase has something of its own to count.

No gate moved. The numbers are the same ones the server was already sending, and
the bar still refuses exactly what it refused yesterday.
