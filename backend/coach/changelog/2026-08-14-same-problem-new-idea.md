title: Same problem, new idea — a third way to close a goal
---

The box that asks what happened now has a third button beside "I achieved it"
and "I'm dropping it". Use it when the idea died and the problem did not, which
is the commonest way this actually goes. It closes the goal exactly as dropping
it would — the same record, the same honest verdict, and a pivot with nobody
spoken to still reads untested — but the next goal you commit is linked to this
one, and Masterji opens it knowing what those weeks found. He will not send you
back to re-interview somebody who already answered.

What carries over is what you learned, never what you earned. The new goal
starts at IDEA with nothing banked and owes its first proof like any other:
writing the new problem statement is one evening at your desk, and it is the
pivot made concrete. The screen tells you what it is carrying, and one press
drops it if you have changed your mind.
