title: The workshop's box and log now behave like the chat's

Three things the room before the goal got wrong, all of them places where it
disagreed with the chat you get after committing. On a phone, Return sent your
turn half-written — and a workshop turn is one of fifteen, so that keystroke
cost you a turn you could not get back. It now does what it does everywhere
else on a phone: adds a line, and Send sends. The conversation also opened on
its oldest three turns, so coming back to the tab showed you the first thing
you said rather than the last thing he said — with his tiebreak, the whole
point of the room, three screens down inside a box you had to know to scroll.
It now opens on the newest turn. And the sentence that closes the room, when
the fifteen are spent, was missing a space in its first two words.
