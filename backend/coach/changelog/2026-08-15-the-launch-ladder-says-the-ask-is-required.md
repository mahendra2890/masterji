title: The launch ladder now says the ask is the rung you cannot skip
---

The launch playbook put the ask — charging, or a committed sign-up — "somewhere
on the ladder", the only rung phrased as a matter of taste. It is the one rung
LAUNCH will not let you skip: the phase does not close until a stranger has
acted on the ask. The ladder now says so, and points at The First Rupee for how
to make it rather than repeating the method. The list of what counts as proof
now marks the same thing: a live post and a written-down rejection both still
count toward the phase, but a LAUNCH made only of posts and rejections does not
close. Nothing about what LAUNCH costs has moved — the playbook was silent about
a requirement you were being held to.
