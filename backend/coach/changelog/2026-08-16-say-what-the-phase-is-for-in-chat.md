title: Say what the new phase is for in chat and it's waiting in the box
---

A phase unlocks in the conversation, and the next question — what will this one
have produced? — used to be one you answered in the chat and then typed again
into the box under the phase. Answer it once now: Masterji writes your line down
in the turn after the unlock, it appears above the box with your words in it,
and one tap puts it in the field. He asks at most once and never chases it — a
phase advances on proofs whatever this says, and leaving it blank costs you
nothing. Nothing is saved until you press Save, and the line the coach quotes
back for the next three weeks is only ever the one you pressed. The box still
works on its own: type it yourself, reword it later, or ignore the draft
entirely.
