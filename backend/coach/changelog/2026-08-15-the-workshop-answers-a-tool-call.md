title: The idea room says what it just did
---

A turn in the idea room that parked a candidate, filled in part of the first
evening's bar, or put a title in the goal box used to say nothing at all — your
message with a blank space under it, and a turn off the meter. Each of those
now comes back with a line: how many ideas are parked and how much room is
left, which parts of a first proof you have given and which are still open, and
that a suggested title is yours to edit and yours to commit. It is in the
conversation as well as on screen, so it is still there when you come back.
