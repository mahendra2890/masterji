title: The week you actually worked is the one read back, not the week you were away
---

The Monday digest read back exactly one week: the one immediately before the
visit that triggered it. So if you worked a full week, disappeared for a
fortnight, and came back, the week it looked at was the empty one you had just
been away for — nothing was written, and the week you were proud of could never
be reached again. When the week just gone is blank, the digest now reaches back
to the last week that actually had check-ins in it, and says which one it is:
*Picking up from the week of 3 Aug — 6 of 7 days complete, 2 accepted toward the
phase.* Naming the date is the point, so it can never be read as a claim about
the fortnight you were gone. Masterji is handed the same week, so he is not
talking about a different one than the message above him. It is still one
message and still once a week — a digest for every week you missed would meet
you on the morning you came back with a stack of them, most saying nothing was
accepted, and that is not a welcome. It reaches back a month at most; past that
you are starting again rather than picking up, and he handles that in
conversation instead.
