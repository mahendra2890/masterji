title: What you worked out before the goal now comes with you
---

The workshop used to hand your goal a title and forget the rest. Now, when
Masterji names the idea the tiebreak landed on, whatever the room actually
established about it — the problem, where those people already are, why you
think so, how you'd get one conversation — comes across with it, and your first
morning starts from what you already said instead of asking for it again. What
the room never got to is named too, because that is what your first evening's
proof is for. Nothing about the bar moves: IDEA still owes one proof, still
filed by you and still judged. The other one-liners you parked are kept as well,
and show up under the closed idea's record — so if this one ends, the thinking
that produced it is still there.
