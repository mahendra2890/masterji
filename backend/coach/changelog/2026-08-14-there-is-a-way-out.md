title: There is a way out — delete your account, with your record on the way past it
---

Until now there was no way to leave. *Delete account* now sits in the account
row beside sign out, and it opens a panel rather than firing: it says exactly
what goes — every goal, every morning you declared and evening you proved, the
whole conversation, the account itself — and offers each goal's record as a file
to keep first, because the record is the thing this product argues is worth
having and you should not lose it on the way out. Then two presses, and it is
done: your evenings stop existing for the app, your email is released so you
could start fresh with the same Google account if you ever wanted to, and the
session ends. Nothing about it is recoverable by signing in again. It is your
diary, and a product that keeps one should always have had a door.
