stopped being the product some releases ago: the draft is written in the
conversation, the presses stand under the words they came from, and on a phone
the app opens on the chat. Every slide now reads in that order — his message

at 0 of 3, telling a builder with two conversations banked to go and have their
first. It now says what the server says at that count — the third one, and the
