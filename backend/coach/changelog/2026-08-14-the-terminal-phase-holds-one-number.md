title: TRACTION holds one number, and nothing is allowed to count it
---

Every other phase gives you something to point at that nobody's opinion
produced — a count, a bar, a door that opens. TRACTION has none of that by
design: it is the end of the ladder and there is nothing left to unlock, which
left the last mile with no shape to it. So you can now name **the one number
that means somebody got the value** — paid deposits, orders through the form —
and log it on the evenings you read it. Masterji is handed the last two readings
and can ask you about the direction; the record keeps the whole line. Nothing
counts it. There is no target, no streak attached to it, and no gate anywhere in
the product that reads it, so a number that falls or sits flat for a week
refuses you nothing and costs you no proof. It is a scoreboard, not a bar. You
can change which number you watch, and the record will say so — the readings you
already took keep the name you took them under, because three evenings of
counting deposits do not become three evenings of counting signups just because
you changed your mind on Friday. That trail is the only consequence there is,
and it is the thing that makes naming a number mean anything.
