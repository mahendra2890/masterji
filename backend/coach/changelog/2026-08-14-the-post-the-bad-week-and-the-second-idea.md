title: Three things the coach can now help with — writing the post, a week you can't give a day to, and the second idea
---

Masterji has a new playbook in LAUNCH on writing the post itself, distilled from
Harry Dry's Marketing Examples: open with the words the people you interviewed
actually used, write for the one room you're posting into rather than "the
public", pick the concrete thing over the clever line, and end with an ask that
costs the reader something small. LAUNCH told you where to post and never how to
write it, and that is the step where the week usually goes quiet. Two existing
playbooks also gained a section. The cadence playbook now covers the week that
isn't week-shaped — exam week, placement season — where the move is to shrink
the slice to thirty minutes rather than pause the loop, and your streak counts
that Tuesday exactly the same as a full Sunday, because it has always counted
declaration and proof and never volume. The over-engineering guide now covers
the second idea, the better one that turns up mid-BUILD on a hard day: write it
down in one line, keep going, and if you are seriously considering the switch,
name what would have to be true about the idea you're on before you look at
whether it is. Nothing about the gates moved.
