title: The LAUNCH refusal counts in the same unit the ladder does
---

Being turned away at the LAUNCH gate came with one line of coaching, and its
arithmetic did not work. It said three rungs was the count and that one of the
three had to be a stranger acting — while the launch playbook you were taught
from lays out a ladder of four rungs, with the ask as the fourth. Three of four,
one of which is the fourth. The gate has never counted rungs: it counts three
accepted proofs, one of them evidence of a stranger acting. The line now says
that, and keeps the ladder as the ladder — four rungs, one a night, climbed
until you have the three. Nothing about what LAUNCH costs has changed; the
sentence describing it was wrong, at the moment it was most worth trusting.
