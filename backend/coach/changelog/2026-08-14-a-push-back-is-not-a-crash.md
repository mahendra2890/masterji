title: A push-back is not painted like a crash
---

A pushed-back proof was drawn in the same red the app uses when something
breaks — a failed request, a dead screen, a chat turn that never landed. It is
not that. The system worked, somebody read your work, and you were asked for
more. It now reads as Masterji speaking, in his own colour, and so do the
earlier tries folded underneath it. Red is for when we broke, not for when he
objected.
