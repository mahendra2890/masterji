title: The same idea parked twice no longer costs you a slot
---

The room before the goal parks at most three one-liners, and that ceiling is
the point of it — once the pile is full the only move left is choosing. If
Masterji parked the same idea twice, though, it took two of those three and
showed up as two identical cards, so you reached the forced choice with two
ideas and a copy of one of them. A one-liner you have already parked is now
kept once, whatever the capitals and spacing. Nothing else about the pile
changes: three is still the limit, a genuinely different idea is still its own
card even if it reads a lot like one above it, and tapping a card still only
fills the box.
