title: Say today's number in chat and it's waiting in the box under Today
---

At Traction you tell Masterji the number — "two people paid today" — and then
scroll down and type 2 into the box under Today. He now writes it down with the
rest of tonight's proof: the figure shows up under his draft, and the button
that fills the proof box fills the number box with it. Only ever the number you
actually said, never one he worked out or carried over from yesterday — if you
did not give him a figure, the box stays empty, which is the right answer. And
it stays a box: edit it, clear it, or ignore all of this and type the number
yourself as before. Nothing is recorded until you press Submit proof, and a
number that comes out wrong is dropped on its own — it can never cost you the
proof it arrived with.
