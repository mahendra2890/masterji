title: Your run and your day count move to the goal card
---

The header carried the streak badge and the lifetime-days count, and on a phone
they cost it a whole third row — 150px of a 640px screen, on every screen,
forever. Worse, that row only appeared once you had banked something: the more
days you kept the promise, the more chrome you got, and the account with nothing
on the record was the one that got the compact header.

Both numbers are now on the goal card, in one line with the days-in-phase count
they are the same kind of fact as: how long this phase has been open, how long
your current run is, how many days are behind you altogether. They sit against
the stepper they describe, and the header is two rows for everyone again.

Nothing about when they appear has changed. No run yet means no badge, a broken
run still shows the best one you had, and the lifetime count still only appears
when it has something to say that the streak doesn't. On the phone, the chat
pane gets the header's third row back.
