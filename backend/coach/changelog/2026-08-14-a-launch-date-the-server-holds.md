title: Name the day you will launch, and which room you will launch into
---

From BUILD onward you can put a date on the card: the day it goes in front of
people, and which room from the launch ladder it goes into. Masterji can see it
from then on, so "nine days out" is something he knows rather than something
you have to keep saying. Moving it is allowed and always will be — but it is
never quietly overwritten. Each change is a new entry, so the card says "moved
1×" and the record keeps the day you first named. That trail is the whole of
what a missed date costs: no proof is refused, no streak breaks, no phase is
held back. Nothing in the gate has ever read this and nothing does now.
