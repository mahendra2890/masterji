title: Delete account is off the dashboard — ask and it still happens
---

The *Delete account* button that arrived on the account row this morning is
gone again. Deleting your account is still something you can have done — ask,
and everything goes exactly as that panel described, with nothing kept back.
What changed is that it is no longer a button sitting beside sign out. Your
record is still yours to take at any time: the export is on each goal, open or
closed, where it has always been.
