title: One proof stays on the record, and now says so
---

The note that reassures you your banked proofs survive a phase change did not
agree with itself when there was exactly one of them — "1 proof from IDEA stay
on the record." IDEA asks for one proof, so that is the sentence every builder
read at their first phase advance, on the screen right after they earned it. It
now reads "stays". Nothing about what is kept has changed; only the sentence
that tells you.
