title: Your goal carries the idea, not just its name
---

Until now the only thing your goal held was its title — a line of text. The
evening you clear IDEA, the problem you wrote up now sits on the goal card under
that title, in your own words, and stays there.

Masterji gets it too, on every turn, separately from your record. That matters
later rather than now: your record carries your ten most recent proofs, so on a
long goal the evening that said what you were building was the first thing to
fall out of what he could see. It no longer does.

Nothing about the gate moved — IDEA still owes its one proof and it is still
judged the same way. You can edit the idea by hand until your first proof is
accepted, the same window in which you can still reword the title.
