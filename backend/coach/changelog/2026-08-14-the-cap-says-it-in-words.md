title: The turn cap says it in words, not in a number
---

There is a ceiling on how many turns you can take in an hour, and when you
reached it the app answered "Masterji said no (429)." — a status code, in the
red it uses when something has broken. Masterji had already written the real
sentence for that moment and you never saw it. You do now: too many at once,
come back to it in a bit. Nothing about the ceiling itself has moved, and a
ceiling has never been able to touch the record — it refuses one request, so
your goal, your streak and every proof you have banked are exactly where you
left them.
