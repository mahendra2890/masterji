title: Two buttons stop shouting — the advance request on day one, and the flattering exit
---

On your first morning the goal card carried exactly one button, and it was
*Request phase advance* at 0/1 under an empty bar. The loudest control on the
main screen was the one whose only possible answer was a refusal — and a refusal
with nothing to add, since the bar above already said 0/1 and the phase hint
already said what the work is. It is now a quiet link, the same weight as *close
this goal*, and it becomes a button again the moment anything is banked. It
still answers when pressed, and the answer at 2/3 — which names *which* piece is
missing — is worth a button, so it gets one.

The second is the retire box. *I achieved it* was the filled button and first in
the row, on a goal whose own counter said the phase was unfinished. Below the
finish line neither exit is the default now; both are outlined, and *I achieved
it* stays first because it is not the shameful option. When the record actually
holds finish-line proof, the card one screen up still lights *Earned. Proof is
on the record.* and a real *Claim the win* — that is where achievement is the
expected move, and it is the database saying so.

Both exits still work from every phase, and the verdict on your record is still
computed from the proofs you earned, never from which button you pressed.
