title: The workshop opens once more, after you have committed
---

The room before the goal answered "I don't have an idea yet". It never answered
"I have one and I no longer believe in it", which is the same sentence four
days later — and until now the only way to get a room for it was to close the
goal first. There is a second door beside "close this goal" now: five turns,
once per goal, with Masterji on your side of the table and the daily loop left
outside. He can see the goal, the phase and everything you have already banked
on it, and there are three things he will put on the table when the
conversation has got somewhere: finish the bar in front of you, sharpen the
wording, or close it today and pick again. Nothing in the room banks, nothing
advances, no proof is drafted and no streak is spent — reconsidering an idea
should not cost you the record of it.
