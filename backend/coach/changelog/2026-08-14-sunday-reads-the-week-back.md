title: The week you just had, read back on Monday
---

The loop is daily and the drift is weekly, so a run of honest days that moved
the gate by nothing looked exactly like a run that moved it. On the first
morning of a new week Masterji now reads the last one back: days complete out
of seven, what was accepted toward the phase, who you spoke to, and any phase
you opened. Nothing is asked for — every number is counted from the check-ins
you already filed — and the same figures go to Masterji, so Monday's
conversation starts knowing what the week held.

A week where nothing was declared writes nothing at all.
