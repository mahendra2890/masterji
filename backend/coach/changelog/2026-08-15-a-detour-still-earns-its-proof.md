title: A day spent off-phase still earns its proof
---

Masterji has always said you are allowed to spend a day on something this phase
is not for — he will tell you it is a detour, and the phase gate is what makes
it cost something. He was not keeping that promise. On an off-phase day the
morning asked you to bring the phase's work tonight rather than the task you
actually declared, and the evening then judged you against that ask — so you
could declare something, do it, file honest proof of it, and be pushed back for
not bringing something you never said you would. That is fixed in both rooms.
The morning now asks for proof of the task you declared, whatever phase it
belongs to; and if the ask ever comes out wrong again, the evening judges what
you declared and did rather than what the ask asked for.
