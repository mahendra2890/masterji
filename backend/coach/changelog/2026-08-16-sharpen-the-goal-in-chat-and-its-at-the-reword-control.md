title: Sharpen the goal's wording in chat and it's waiting at the reword control
---

Talking your goal into a sharper sentence happens in the conversation, and
until now the sentence then had to be typed a second time: leave the chat, find
the reword control on the goal card, and write it out again from memory. Now
Masterji writes down the sharper wording you arrived at, it appears under the
goal in your words, and one tap opens the reword box already holding it. It is
your sharpening he writes down, not his preferred phrasing of your idea — the
goal is the thing every proof you file is evidence for, so the words stay
yours. Nothing is renamed until you press Save wording, and the same window
applies as always: the wording is yours to change until the first proof banks
against it, and after that it stays as it is. Draft one before that evening and
press it after, and you will get the same refusal you would have got typing it
— the suggestion never outruns the record. The reword control still works on
its own: type it yourself, or ignore the draft.
