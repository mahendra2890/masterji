title: File it where he drafted it — Declare it and Submit proof now stand in the chat
---

When Masterji writes today's task or tonight's proof out of your conversation,
the draft now appears in the chat with its button on it: **Declare it** under a
drafted task, **Submit proof** — link and screenshot included — under tonight's.
On a phone that ends the errand the draft used to create, where the thing you
had just finished landed in the pane you were not looking at.

Nothing else moved. It is the same press writing the same row, so nothing is
ever recorded until you make it; Today still carries the meter, the streak, the
record and the refusals, and its own controls still work exactly as they did. A
draft that is not finished still shows what it is missing, and filing it anyway
is still your call. The card is only ever the offer that is live right now — the
moment you file it, or he rewrites it, or the day turns over, it stops being a
button, so nothing in yesterday's scrollback can file anything into today.
