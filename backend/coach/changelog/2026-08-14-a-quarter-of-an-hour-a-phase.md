title: The playbooks say how long they actually take
---

Masterji's playbooks are public, and the tour told you they were about ten
minutes of reading. They had grown to about forty-nine, and four places in this
repository were still quoting the old number.

The claim now says the thing that was always the useful one: **your phase's
playbooks are about a quarter of an hour**, and that is the whole of what
Masterji is handed when he judges tonight's proof. The full corpus, if you want
to audit all of it rather than the shelf you are standing at, is under an hour.

Nothing about the playbooks themselves changed, and none of them was removed —
only the sentence describing how long they take. A test now measures the folder
and fails if either number stops being true, so the next playbook that lands
cannot quietly leave the promise behind.
